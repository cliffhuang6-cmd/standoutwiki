**Target Domain**: `https://standoutwiki.org/`
**Target Audience**: Roblox STAND OUT players (English-speaking global community)
**Primary Search Engine**: Google Search (SERP Top 1 Intent Fulfillment)

---

## 1. Strategy & Content Architecture

To prevent keyword cannibalization and maximize topical authority, each search cluster is assigned a **single authoritative canonical page**:
* Core entity and moveset queries for any Stand (`stand out star platinum`, `how to get star platinum`, `star platinum moveset`) map exclusively to that Stand's detail page (`/stands/star-platinum/`).
* Obtainment guides with multi-step evolution trees (such as SPTW or TWAU) or complex quests (Hamon, Vampire) receive dedicated, step-by-step walkthrough guides with bidirectional links to the Stand/Sub-ability pages.
* Interactive tool pages serve utility queries (`arrow calculator`, `stat builder`, `rokakaka mixer`) while retaining rich indexable content for search crawlers.
